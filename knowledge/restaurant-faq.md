# Harbour & Herb FAQ

Opening hours: Tuesday to Sunday, 11:00 to 22:00. Closed Mondays.

Standard reservation duration: 90 minutes.

Cancellation policy: Cancel or reschedule at least 2 hours before the reservation start time.

Special requests: Window seat, garden table, birthday notes, high chair, and accessibility needs can be added when booking.

Seating: Window two-tops, garden tables, booths for six, and a private table for eight.

Party size: 1 to 20 guests. Larger parties should call the restaurant.

Booking: Guests choose date, time, and guest count. The system assigns an available table with enough capacity.

Check-in: Please arrive on time. The manager marks the table occupied at check-in.

No-show: Confirmed reservations that do not arrive may be marked no-show.

Feedback: After a completed visit, guests can leave a 1-5 rating and comment.
